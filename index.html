About Godded Seed

A generation inspired by God to give effective leadership to the world

Godded Seed means God's offspring. It's an idea of a generation inspired by God to give effective leadership to the world.

The Godded Seed Community is a learning community designed to inspire, and equip you for heightened capacity, influence, and impact in your life's calling. We do that by providing sound learning opportunities, a platform for collaboration, growth, and life-changing events underpinned on kingdom truths.

The Godded Seed Confab is our Enterprise, Leadership, and Ministry Skyrocketing Conference. A transformational experience designed to activate the next phase of your life, skyrocket your growth, and raise a generation that will give effective leadership to the world.
